Hospital Services

Departments and medical services available at Bugema University Hospital.

General Medicine

Diagnosis and treatment of common illnesses, chronic conditions, and preventive care for all age groups.

Walk-in & Appointments

Paediatrics

Comprehensive healthcare for infants, children, and adolescents including immunisations and growth monitoring.

Appointments Required

Obstetrics & Gynaecology

Maternal care, antenatal clinics, delivery services, and gynaecological consultations.

Appointments Required

Dental Services

Routine check-ups, fillings, extractions, oral hygiene advice, and dental X-rays.

Appointments Required

Ophthalmology

Eye examinations, vision testing, treatment of eye conditions, and referrals for specialist surgery.

Appointments Required

Mental Health

Counselling, psychiatric assessments, and support for students and staff managing stress and mental health challenges.

Confidential & By Appointment

Radiology & Imaging

X-rays, ultrasound scans, and diagnostic imaging to support clinical diagnosis.

Referral Required

Laboratory Services

Blood tests, urinalysis, malaria RDTs, HIV testing, and other diagnostic investigations.

Walk-in & Referral

Emergency & Casualty

24-hour emergency care for accidents, acute illnesses, and urgent medical conditions.

24 / 7 Walk-in

Pharmacy

Dispensing of prescribed medications, over-the-counter medicines, and patient counselling on drug use.

Open Daily

Physiotherapy

Rehabilitation for injuries, post-operative recovery, and management of musculoskeletal conditions.

Appointments Required

HIV / AIDS Clinic

Voluntary counselling and testing, ART management, and adherence support in a confidential setting.

Confidential & Free

How to Access These Services

{% if session.role == 'patient' %}

After submitting your appointment request, please wait for it to be confirmed. You can track the status on your dashboard. Contact reception if you need help.

{% elif session.role == 'staff' %}

Book appointments for patients using the Appointments section. Register new patients using Register Patient. Patients can also log in and book on their own.

{% elif session.role == 'doctor' %}

Appointments assigned to you appear on your schedule. Update statuses after consultations to keep patient records accurate.

{% elif session.role == 'admin' %}

You have full access to manage all services, doctors, patients, staff, and appointments from the navigation above.

{% endif %}
